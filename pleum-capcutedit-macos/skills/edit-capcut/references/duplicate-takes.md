# Duplicate takes

Detect false starts, explicit/prefix restarts, complete repeats, corrections, abandoned phrases, and repeated thoughts. Prefer complete, fluent, clean, confident takes with important-content containment. Preserve natural emphasis and materially different facts. Process safe groups automatically; skip unsafe groups independently. Ripple descending or through a proven cumulative map.

A restart marker is never sufficient by itself. When evidence relies on shared
tokens rather than strong prefix or sequence similarity, require at least 60%
shorter-take containment as well as the minimum matching-token count. This
prevents natural phrases such as `คำถามจริงๆ ที่ผมอยากถามคือ...` from being
paired with later, semantically unrelated story beats.
